I am designing Kity Farm, an educational kids' cooking café
concept for San Jose, California. The concept combines an
urban farm with a cooking café where children can harvest
produce and learn to cook.

The target users are children aged 4-10 and their parents
in Silicon Valley, where there is high demand for educational
and nature-based experiences for kids.

Key features include:
Farm-to-dining program (harvesting + cooking classes)
Farm visit experiences linked to local farms
Mobile app for booking classes and tracking activities
Brand identity combining farm and city concepts

My main goals are to make the experience feel educational
but fun, and to communicate the brand clearly to both
parents (who decide) and children (who experience it).

I am currently at the concept and branding stage.
